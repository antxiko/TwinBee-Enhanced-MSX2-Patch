## Who owns what

**The game is not ours.** *Twin Bee* was published by **Konami** for the MSX
in 1986; it is a 32 KB cartridge. All rights over the game remain with their
holders.

**The music driver is not ours either.** The SCC music comes from the
*Konami Game Collection Vol. 3* (Konami, 1988): its sound driver and its
music data travel inside this patch, adapted only so that they run from the
MegaROM and play on the standard SCC. All rights over them remain with
Konami.

**The graphics are Guantxip's**: the five stage maps, the sprites and the
bosses were drawn and adapted by Guantxip for this project.

**What is ours** is the rest: the new scenery and sprite engine, the
adaptation of the driver, the intro, the tools and the documentation, published
under the licence in `LICENSE`.

## What is in this repository

An **unofficial patch**, `ips/twinbee_enhanced_msx2.ips`: a difference file
that, applied to your own dump of the cartridge, produces the enhanced
MegaROM. It does not contain the original game, but it does contain the new
code, Guantxip's graphics and the Game Collection music driver described
above. Neither the original cartridge nor the patched one is distributed.

The work behind it rests on the commented disassembly of the cartridge,
published separately for the preservation, study and documentation of a
title that is part of MSX software history.

If you worked on *Twin Bee* or on the *Konami Game Collection*, or hold
rights over them, and you would rather this material were not published,
**say so and it comes down, no argument**. The intent of this work is the
opposite of harming you: it is a tribute to the game.
